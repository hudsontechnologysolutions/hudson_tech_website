The world has changed. Have you?

AI is rewriting entire industries. Customers expect instant, mobile-first experiences. Your competitors — even the small ones — are automating what used to take your team all week. The organizations that thrive in this environment aren't the biggest; they're the ones that move fastest. And right now, speed runs on software.

Yet most small businesses and non-profits are still running on spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and manual processes that were outdated five years ago. Every hour your staff spends on data entry, copy-paste reporting, or chasing information across inboxes is an hour not spent on the work that actually grows your organization. That gap isn't standing still — it's widening every quarter.

Custom software closes that gap. One well-built application can automate your most time-consuming workflows, give your team real-time visibility into operations, and create the kind of seamless digital experience your clients and community now expect. It's the same operational advantage Fortune 500 companies have relied on for decades — built to fit your budget, your workflow, and your goals.

The question isn't whether your organization needs technology — it's how long you can afford to wait. We help you stop falling behind and start building the tools that put you ahead.

Software & Web Development

From marketing websites to custom web and mobile applications, we deliver end-to-end software that helps your organization win customers, automate operations, and scale efficiently. As a boutique agency, every project gets focused attention from a senior engineer — no juniors, no handoffs, no overhead. You get the rigor of an enterprise engineering team with the responsiveness of a single dedicated partner.

  • Make a strong first impression. Modern, mobile-friendly websites that build trust the moment someone lands — fast, polished, and crafted to represent your organization with the credibility it deserves.
  • Cut operating costs. Automate repetitive work — data entry, reporting, scheduling, invoicing — so your team can focus on mission-critical priorities instead of manual busywork.
  • Unlock new revenue. Subscription platforms, online booking, secure checkout, and e-commerce flows — purpose-built to open revenue streams your current setup can't support.
  • Reach your audience anywhere. Web and mobile apps, push notifications, and real-time tools meet customers on the device they already use, driving engagement and retention.

Lead Developer & Founder

Roberto Rodas-Herndon

As the sole developer behind Hudson Technology Solutions, Roberto brings the engineering rigor he honed at Pinterest, PSEG, and PowerOptions directly to every client. No accounts managers, no offshore handoffs — just one engineer fully invested in your project from kickoff to launch.

Hudson Technology Solutions was founded by Roberto Rodas-Herndon, a Boston University Computer Science graduate. While at Boston University, he served as President of the Hack4Impact chapter (2021–2023), leading a student-run team that built free software for non-profits — the same mission that inspired the founding of this agency.

From navigating Pinterest's codebase at their headquarters in San Francisco alongside senior engineers, to providing technical insights across the enterprise at PSEG, we bring big-company engineering rigor to organizations that need it most — those shaping their communities.

Every project ships with modern architecture and a focus on real-world impact. We're also bilingual (English & Spanish), which helps us serve Hudson County's diverse communities directly in their language.
